# AEGI Public Repository Scope

## Purpose
This repository contains only material explicitly approved for public disclosure: demonstrations, examples, public evidence artifacts and public technical documentation.

## Allowed
- approved public examples;
- synthetic/non-production demonstrations;
- public evidence summaries;
- public schemas or interfaces explicitly cleared for release;
- public verification instructions;
- materials consistent with the approved AEGI claim boundary.

## Forbidden
- customer data or customer-specific mappings;
- private signing keys or secrets;
- restricted CTS/golden vectors;
- patent-sensitive implementation details;
- exploit-sensitive failure material;
- unapproved performance, production, regulatory or compliance claims;
- internal pricing or partner economics.

## Publication rule
Material becomes public only after disclosure review. Presence in another AEGI repository or Google Drive does not imply public-release approval.

## Evidence boundary
Synthetic or benchmark evidence must retain its declared scope and limitations. Public artifacts must not imply production fraud reduction, regulatory endorsement or autonomous banking authority unless separately and explicitly supported.
